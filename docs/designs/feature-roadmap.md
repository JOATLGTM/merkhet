## 0A. Premise

The PRD's problem is three questions staff can't answer today: where is every project, who owns the current phase, and what is due when. The board answers the first two. The third is only partly answered: dates live on each project's phases, and nothing tells a person what is due for *them* or what is late. The PRD success test is "the team stops maintaining the spreadsheet". A spreadsheet survives when it is the only place someone can see their own due list.

Clients are the second audience. Since 2026-09-27 they receive phase emails automatically. Their experience is the email itself, and the studio's record of what each client was told.

Do-nothing cost: staff keep a side list of due dates, late phases are noticed in conversation, and there is no in-app record of what a client was emailed beyond the raw outbox table.

## Accepted scope: specification

### H1: real studio contact details in client email
See Step 0 findings. Blocked only on the three values. The same `FIRM` values also appear in internal and auth email footers (`lib/email/layout.ts:59-61`, `lib/email/render.ts:119-121`); that is intended.

### F2: late and due-soon flags (D39 A)
- **When a flag shows.** The project is not completed or archived, `current_phase` is set (Pending, backlog and Active-holding cards show nothing), the current phase is not completed or skipped, the project is not on hold, and the current phase has a `planned_end`.
- **Day count.** Days = `differenceInCalendarDays(parseISO(planned_end), today)` (parsed as `dateUrgency` does) using the same local-date "today" the calendar's today marker uses (`calendar-view.tsx:156-157`).
  - Days < 0: "Phase late Nd" in `--redline`, N = −days.
  - Days = 0: "Phase due today" in `--warn`.
  - Days 1 to 3: "Phase due in Nd" in `--warn`.
  - Days > 3: nothing.
- **Card slot.** In the top row of `project-card.tsx:84-91`, right-aligned before the on-hold icon (the two never show together, since on-hold cards get no flag). Label-size mono text, no fill. The visible label is `aria-hidden`, and an `sr-only` span reads "late 1 day" / "late N days", "due today" or "due in 1 day" / "due in N days" (singular for 1).
- **Relation to the existing target-date colour.** Cards already colour the client's target completion date with `dateUrgency` (red when past, amber within 14 days). That stays unchanged: it is the project's overall deadline, while F2 is the current phase's deadline. F2's badge carries a "Phase" prefix ("Phase late 2d", "Phase due today", "Phase due in 2d") so the two signals read differently when both show.
- **Calendar.** Only the current phase's bar of a flagged project changes, and only when late: the bar's existing border (`border border-surface`, `calendar-view.tsx:193`) changes colour to `--redline`; no fill change, so label contrast stays. Due-soon bars and fallback bars for undated projects are unchanged. The bar's existing `sr-only` span (`calendar-view.tsx:206-208`) gains ", late N days" (singular for 1).
